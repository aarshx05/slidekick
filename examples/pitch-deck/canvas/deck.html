One roof sells 140,000 lb a year at $2.95, which is $413k. After labour, inputs and the roof lease, $124k is left: a 30% margin and a 26-month payback on a $270k build.

How we sized it

9,400 stores in the top 50 metros have a flat, load-rated roof over 25,000 sq ft. At $413k a roof, that is $3.9B.

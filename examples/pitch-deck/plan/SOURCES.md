## Brand kit (made for this example)
- `brand/assets/fernway-*.svg`: the Fernway mark (gable and leaf) and lockups, drawn for this example. The wordmark is Fraunces SemiBold converted to outlines with fontTools, so it renders without the font.
- `brand/brand.css`: the kit's source tokens (palette and type), ingested with `deck.py brand brand/ -p .`. `brand/brand.json` was then corrected by hand (theme roles, chart palette, rules).
- `brand/fonts/Fraunces-*.ttf`: Fraunces by the Fraunces Project Authors (Undercase Type), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-Fraunces.txt`). Fetched from Google Fonts with `deck.py fonts fetch "Fraunces" --install`.
- `brand/fonts/DMSans-*.ttf`: DM Sans by the DM Sans Project Authors (Colophon Foundry), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-DMSans.txt`). Fetched from Google Fonts with `deck.py fonts fetch "DM Sans" --install`.
